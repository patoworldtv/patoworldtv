Privacy Policy for PatoworldTV

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

At PatoworldTV, accessible from https://patoworldtv.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by PatoworldTV and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit or interact with our platform, we may collect certain information, including:

  • Account Information: When site administrators register or log in, we store authentication credentials and metadata required for platform security and content management.
  • Content & Media Data: Articles, titles, categories, comments, and uploaded media files (photos or videos) submitted through our administrative dashboard.
  • Log Files: Like many standard web hosting services, our servers automatically log standard technical data such as IP addresses, browser types, timestamps, and referring pages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and automated news feeds.
  • Publish and syndicate interactive news updates across authorized social media management tools (such as Buffer and connected social platforms).
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our website features and content offerings.
  • Communicate with you for support, updates, or administrative purposes.

3. Automated Publishing & Third-Party Services

PatoworldTV utilizes automated workflows (via RSS feeds and Make.com integrations) to syndicate public posts to social platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, and TikTok via Buffer. Only data explicitly published on our website feed is transmitted through these authorized APIs.

4. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us through email at support@patoworldtv.com.
